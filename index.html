Dice roller

Built-in rule reminders

Initial scores: Skill is 1D6 + 6. Stamina is 2D6 + 12. Luck is 1D6 + 6.

Attack round: Monster rolls 2D6 + Skill. You roll 2D6 + current Skill. Higher attack strength wounds the other side for 2 Stamina. A draw means both miss.

Test Luck: Roll 2D6. Equal to or less than current Luck is lucky. Higher is unlucky. Luck drops by 1 after every test.

Provisions: Eating one meal restores 4 Stamina, cannot exceed Initial Stamina, and reduces provisions by 1.

Potions: Skill restores Skill. Strength restores Stamina. Fortune adds 1 to Initial Luck, then restores Luck to that new value.
