Quick Rules

Initial Skill: 1D6 + 6. Initial Stamina: 2D6 + 12. Initial Luck: 1D6 + 6.

Attack round: roll 2D6 + Skill for both sides. Higher score wounds the other side for 2 Stamina. A draw means both miss.

Test Luck: roll 2D6. Equal to or less than current Luck is lucky. After every test, reduce Luck by 1.

Provisions: start with 10. Eating one meal restores 4 Stamina, but not above initial Stamina.
