Quick Rules

Starting hero: Skill = 1D6 + 6. Stamina = 2D6 + 12. Luck = 1D6 + 6.

Combat: roll 2D6 + monster Skill, then 2D6 + your Skill. Higher attack strength wins. Loser normally takes 2 Stamina damage.

Luck: roll 2D6. Equal to or below current Luck succeeds. Reduce Luck by 1 after every test.

Provisions: one meal restores 4 Stamina and costs 1 provision, but Stamina normally cannot exceed Initial Stamina.
